# Hi, I'm Chip 👋

I'm a product designer with 12 years of experience. Through Made By Chip, I embed AI-assisted design workflows into product teams and document what actually happens: the prompts, the failures, the iterations.

Formerly Lead Product Designer at Grab (GrabMaps), where I led Driver Voice AI for 1M+ drivers across 6 countries. Co-founder of CarinMED, a mobile-first medical SaaS.
